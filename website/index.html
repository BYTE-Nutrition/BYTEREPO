How it works

You talk.
BYTE answers.

BYTE isn't passive. It's a back-and-forth conversation that happens while your hands are busy. Ask anything. Say what you're adding. Get instant, specific guidance — no Googling, no pausing, no burned garlic.

You said "I'm sautéing onions on high heat, how long should I keep going?"
BYTE About 4 more minutes. Look for translucent edges and slight browning. Turn to medium-high now — high will burn the sugars before they caramelize.
You said "Can I swap the olive oil for butter in this?"
⚠ BYTE — heads up Yes, but lower the heat first. Butter burns at 300°F — you're currently above that. Equal substitution works, but you'll get more browning and a richer flavor.
You said "Adding garlic now"
BYTE Good timing. Stir continuously — garlic goes from raw to burnt in under 60 seconds at this temp.
